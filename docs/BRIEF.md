# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Project name:** NEXUS

**Tagline:** Explore. Learn. Build.

**Project purpose:**

NEXUS is a free technology knowledge and learning platform where people can explore, research and learn about different areas of Information Technology.

The website should bring together useful learning materials from across the internet, including:

- YouTube videos and playlists.
- Technical blogs and articles.
- Official documentation.
- Free online courses.
- Research papers.
- GitHub repositories.
- Practical projects.
- Latest technology updates.

The goal is to make technology learning easier, more organised and accessible to everyone.

The website should be suitable for:
- Complete beginners.
- University students.
- Self-taught developers.
- Software engineers.
- IT professionals.
- Anyone interested in technology.

**Important:** NEXUS is not just a link directory. It should organise knowledge into meaningful topics, learning paths and useful resources.
